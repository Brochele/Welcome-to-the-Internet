What is the Internet?

The Internet is a link between computers, wether its data servers or Personal Computers (PCs) It allows for different computers to talk to eachother and exchange data. Earlier websites used just Hyper Text Markup Language, or HTML. Later websites introduced Java, Javascript, and CSS (Cascading Styles Sheet). Most times the Internet is used to answer questions and work to find new informtion. It can also be used for communicating, such as E-mail. The internet started as a small project, but has grown into and everyday life tool.
